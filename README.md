# Customer Churn Prediction using Machine Learning
**Live demo:** <https://customer-churn-prediction-nphytgjal47jyxof5toecd.streamlit.app/>

Predicts which telecom customers are likely to leave, explains why, and turns the predictions into actions a retention team can use.

## Problem
Losing customers is expensive. The goal is to flag at-risk customers early (binary classification: churn / no churn) and identify the factors that drive churn.

## Dataset
[Telco Customer Churn](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn): 7,043 customers, 21 columns, 26.5% churn (imbalanced).

## Approach
1. **Cleaning:** `TotalCharges` held blank strings for 11 customers with tenure 0 (never billed); replaced with 0 and converted to numeric.
2. **EDA:** churn rates by contract, internet service and payment method.
3. **Feature engineering:** number of services, tenure group, auto-pay flag. `TotalCharges` was dropped because it overlaps tenure x monthly charges (multicollinearity).
4. **Imbalance:** handled with class weights.
5. **Models:** Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost compared with 5-fold stratified cross-validation.
6. **Threshold tuning:** the cutoff was chosen on cross-validated training predictions only (no test leakage).
7. **Explainability:** logistic coefficients, Random Forest importances and SHAP.

## Results
| Model | CV ROC-AUC |
|---|---|
| Logistic Regression | 0.846 ± 0.011 |
| Random Forest | 0.820 ± 0.014 |
| XGBoost | 0.847 ± 0.011 |

Logistic Regression and XGBoost are statistically tied; **Logistic Regression was selected for interpretability.**

Test set (threshold 0.58): churn precision 0.55, recall 0.73, F1 0.62, ROC-AUC 0.841.

## Key findings
- **Month-to-month contracts** churn at 42.7% versus 11.3% (one year) and 2.8% (two year).
- **Fiber optic** customers churn at 41.9% versus 19.0% for DSL.
- **Electronic check** payers churn at 45.3%, versus about 15-19% for other methods.
- **New customers** (low tenure) are the highest risk; churn falls steadily as tenure grows.
- SHAP, logistic coefficients and EDA all agree on these drivers.

## Business recommendations
1. Incentivize one- and two-year contracts.
2. Focus retention efforts on the first year.
3. Investigate fiber optic price and quality.
4. Move electronic-check payers to auto-pay.
5. Use model risk segments (Low / Medium / High) to prioritize outreach.

## Business impact (illustrative)
Assuming 500 revenue per churned customer, 50 per retention offer and a 30% save rate, the model reduces estimated loss by about 8.5% on the test set. These figures are assumptions, not dataset facts, and the gain grows if offers are cheaper or more effective.

## Limitations
- Single dataset snapshot; no time dimension.
- Financial figures are assumptions.
- Moderate precision (0.55): about half of flagged customers would not have churned.
